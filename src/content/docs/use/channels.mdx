## Channel types

| Type | Who gets in | Encrypted with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open | Anyone | Nothing |
| Protected | Anyone with the password | The password |
| Closed | Addresses the owner adds | Keys only members receive |
| Gated | Holders of a token or an NFT | Keys only members receive |
| Paid | Anyone with an active subscription | Keys only members receive |

How each type is enforced: [Access and gates](/protocol/access/).

## Join

- **From Explore:** open a channel and press **Join**.
- **From a link:** channel links look like `https://app.pombo.cc/#/channel/…`.
- **By ID:** **Join Channel** on the web, **Join a channel** on Android, with the password if there is one.

Protected channels ask for the password. Closed, Gated and Paid channels open a join screen that shows what the channel asks for and whether you have it:

- **Closed:** ask the owner to add your address, then press **Check Again**.
- **Gated:** hold the token or NFT shown, then press **Enter Channel**.
- **Paid:** press **Pay** with the price shown.

<Aside type="caution">
A link to a Protected channel is an invite that carries the password. Anyone who gets the link can read the channel.
</Aside>

## Paid subscriptions

- Paying extends your subscription from its current end. The payment goes straight to the owner.
- In the last 3 days a banner shows **Subscription ends in …** with **Renew**.
- When it ends, the channel locks until you renew. There is no grace period.

## Create a channel

<Steps>

1. Open **Create Channel** and pick a type.
2. Name it, then fill in what the type needs: the password, the member addresses, the token or NFT and minimum balance, or the payment token, price and period (30 days by default).
3. For Closed, Gated and Paid, pick **Identity on the wire**: **Sealed** or **Visible**. It cannot change later.
4. Keep **Listed** on to show the channel in Explore, with a description, language and category. It is on by default. Closed channels are never listed.
5. Turn on **Read-Only** if only you, and your moderators, should post. Members can still react.
6. Pick the **Storage Provider** and **Message Retention**: 1 day to 1 year, 6 months by default.
7. Check **Est. cost** and create. Keep the app open until every transaction is done.

</Steps>

| | Sealed (default) | Visible |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who sees each author | Members only | Anyone, forever |
| Removed members | Can still fill storage with junk until you **Reset Publish Key**, a paid action | Rejected by readers, and by Pombo's storage nodes within 10 minutes |

## Manage a channel

Open the channel's details. The web shows them as tabs: **Info**, **Members**, **Notifications**, **Storage**, **Moderation** and **Delete**.

| To | Go to |
| --- | --- |
| Edit the name or description | **Info › Edit channel info** |
| Change the image | **Info › Upload image** |
| Add members (Closed) | **Members › Add Members** |
| Remove a member or make them a moderator | **Members**, then the member's menu |
| Change retention or add a storage provider | **Storage** |
| Hide, pin, ban | [Moderation](/use/moderation/) |
| Delete the channel for everyone | **Delete** |

Editing info, members, moderators or storage, and deleting, are Polygon transactions. A storage provider added later keeps only what is published after it joins.

## Leave

**Leave Channel** in the channel menu. You can join again later.
