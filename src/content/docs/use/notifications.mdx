## Turn them on

<Steps>

1. **Settings › Notifications › Push Notifications**. Allow notifications when the browser asks; Android 13 and newer ask when the app starts.
2. Turn them on for each channel: **Notifications On** in its details, or the **Notifications** tab on the web.
3. Turn on **Direct Messages** once your DM inbox exists.

</Steps>

**Channel Invites** controls the alerts for invitations to channels. On Android, a conversation's details can mute that one DM.

## What they show

| From | Title | Text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open channels | The channel name | A preview of the message |
| Protected, Closed, Gated and Paid channels | The channel name | *New message* |
| Direct messages, Android | The sender's nickname, ENS name or address | A preview of the message |
| Direct messages, web | *Pombo* | *You have a new message* |

## On the web

Open and Protected channels notify with no Pombo tab open. Closed, Gated and Paid channels and direct messages need a Pombo tab open, even in the background: checking them needs your key, and the key never leaves the page.

<Aside>
On iPhone and iPad, add Pombo to the Home Screen first: Safari only notifies for web apps installed there.
</Aside>

## What the relay learns

The relay that wakes your device sees no message, no sender, no channel and no recipient. Each wake goes to every device registered under a tag that many channels share, so the device it is meant for is one in that crowd (k-anonymity). Each device then checks for itself whether anything is new. How it works: [Push notifications](/protocol/push/).
