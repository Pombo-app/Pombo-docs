The owner moderates every channel. Closed, Gated and Paid channels can also have moderators: the owner appoints them in **Members**, from the member's menu, with **Moderator**.

Every action works at one of three levels:

- **Override:** a declaration that Pombo apps apply. The message stays in storage, and an app that ignores overrides still shows it.
- **Erase:** storage providers that support erasing delete the message. Cannot be undone.
- **Access:** the gate contract and a new channel key cut the account off.

## The message menu

Right-click or long-press a message on the web; long-press it on Android.

| Action | Who | What it does | Level |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Edit Message** | The author | Changes the text | Override |
| **Delete Message** | The author | Removes it. Outside Sealed channels, Pombo also erases it from storage when it can; the dialog says whether it will | Override, and Erase when possible |
| **Pin Message** | The owner | Keeps it at the top of the channel | Override |
| **Hide Message** | The owner, moderators | Hides it; **Unhide Message** shows it again | Override |
| **Erase from storage** | The owner, moderators | Deletes it from every provider that can erase | Erase |
| **Ban User** | The owner, moderators | See below | |

The owner removes their own messages with **Hide Message**. Pinning and hiding are free.

## Banning

**Ban User** offers three choices.

| Choice | What it does | Who, and cost | Level |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Hide their messages** | Hides everything they wrote, before and after | Free, reversible | Override |
| **Cut their access** | Bans them on the gate and rotates the channel key: they cannot write, and cannot read what follows | The owner; Closed, Gated and Paid only; one transaction | Access |
| **Erase their messages from storage** | Deletes their messages and files from providers that can | The owner, with the first choice | Erase |

In Open and Protected channels there is no gate, so a ban can only hide. If the key cannot rotate at once, the owner's app rotates it the next time it connects.

**Moderation › Banned Members** lists bans, with **Unban**. Lifting a ban that cut access takes one transaction. Lifting a hide-only ban is free.

## Moderators' actions

What a moderator hides or bans takes effect at once. The owner then sees **Confirm Moderator Actions** in **Moderation**: confirming is free, and keeps those actions even if the moderator is dismissed later.

## Advanced

On the web, the **Advanced** section of **Moderation** holds these buttons, for the owner of a Closed, Gated or Paid channel.

| Button | Use it to | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Rotate Now** | Issue a new channel key now. Anyone without access stops reading new messages. The owner's app also rotates the key weekly | Free |
| **Reset Publish Key** | Sealed channels: cut off an ex-member who keeps writing junk to storage | Two transactions |
| **Reset Interactions Key** | Sealed channels: bring back reactions and presence when no device holds their key, or cut off an ex-member who kept it | One transaction |

## Key Responder

New members get the channel keys from a member's device that is online. **Key Responder** keeps one of yours answering:

- **Web:** in the channel's **Notifications** tab, for the owner and moderators, while a Pombo tab is open.
- **Android:** **Background Key Responder**, for the owner and moderators, even with the app closed.

<Aside type="caution" title="Moderation state is too large">
Pins, hidden messages and bans are stored together as one state. Pombo splits a large state into up to four parts automatically. Past that, it refuses to publish and asks you to unpin some messages.
</Aside>
