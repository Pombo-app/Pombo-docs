## Channels and messages

| You see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| *Waiting for channel keys…* | Wait. Keys come from another member's device, so one must be online. |
| *Incorrect password for this channel* | Ask the owner for the password, or for an invite link. |
| *No active subscription*, *Subscription expired* | Press **Subscribe**, or **Renew** if the subscription has expired. |
| *A moderator removed your access to this channel* | Only the owner can give it back. |
| *Channel history is temporarily unavailable* | Reopen the channel. If it says the node has not caught up with your access, wait: it retries by itself. |
| *The storage node did not accept this read* | Check that your device's clock is right. |
| *No key for the current epoch* (owner) | Press **Rotate** to write again. |
| *This channel's moderation state is too large to publish* | Unpin some messages. |

## Direct messages

| You see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| *This user has not enabled DMs yet* | They need to open Pombo and create their inbox. If you know they have one, try again later. |
| *Create DM inbox first to enable sync* (web) | Create your DM inbox: see [Direct messages](/use/direct-messages/). |

## Payments

| You see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| *No POL in wallet*, *Not enough POL for the network fee* | Top up: **Wallet › Fund with MetaMask**. |
| *Low POL balance* | The balance may not cover the estimate. Top up, or press **Create anyway**. |
| *Transaction conflict, try again* | Another transaction from your account was pending. Try again. |
| *Network error, try again* | Check your connection and your RPC endpoints, below. |

## Settings that help

- **Settings › Networks › Polygon RPC** picks which endpoints Pombo uses, in order. **Add custom endpoint** adds your own; **Test all** checks them. On the web, *Your RPC endpoints disagree on access to this channel* means one of them may be compromised: check them here.
- **Settings › Networks › The Graph API**: Explore and channel lookups use a shared, rate-limited key. If Explore is slow or empty, add your own from [thegraph.com/studio](https://thegraph.com/studio/).
- **Settings › DM Inbox** shows the state of your inbox; **Repair** fixes what it can.
- **Sync Now** brings in what your other devices changed. See [Backup and devices](/use/backup/).
