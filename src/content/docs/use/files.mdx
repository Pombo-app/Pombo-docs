---
title: Files and media
description: Send images, videos and files, peer to peer or through storage.
---

The attach menu beside the message box has **Image**, **File** and **Video**. Files and videos go one of two ways:

| | P2P | Storage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Travels | Straight from devices that hold the file | Through the channel's storage providers |
| Available | While a device holding it is online | As long as the channel keeps its messages |

A P2P file can be up to 500 MB. On the web, an image larger than 1 MB is converted to fit; a GIF can be up to 5 MB.

## Transfers

The **Transfers** button in the header opens the list of active downloads and seeds. Each download has a pause control, titled **Pause download** and **Resume download** on the web. **Show inactive** brings back finished ones. On Android, a **File transfers active** notification keeps transfers going in the background.
## Seeding

Once you send or download a P2P file, your device serves it to others while Pombo is open, and its bubble shows a **seeding...** status. The more members hold a file, the longer it stays available.
