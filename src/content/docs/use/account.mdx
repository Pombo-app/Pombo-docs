Your account is a Polygon key pair. Its address is who you are in Pombo: others see it, write to it and add it to channels.

## Address and name

- **Settings › Account** shows your **Account Address**. Tap it to copy.
- **Display Name** is what others see, up to 18 characters.
- If your address has a primary ENS name, Pombo uses it in place of the display name and marks it **ENS Verified**. Others can start a DM with that name.

## Wallet

**Wallet** lists your POL, DATA and USDC. **Fund with MetaMask** opens MetaMask, the browser extension or the Android app, to top up this address.

| Costs POL | Free |
| --- | --- |
| Creating a channel | Reading and writing |
| Creating your DM inbox, once | Reactions |
| Editing a channel's info or storage | Pinning and hiding messages |
| Adding and removing members, moderators | Rotating a channel key |
| Cutting someone's access | Device sync |
| Paying a subscription | |

Before creating a channel or an inbox, Pombo shows **Est. cost** and warns you if the balance looks too low.

## Private key

**Settings › Security › Private Key**. Unlock it with your password on the web, or your screen lock on Android, then **Hold to Copy (2s)**. On Android the key leaves the clipboard after 60 seconds.

<Aside type="danger">
Anyone with this key has full control of your account.
</Aside>

## Delete an account

**Settings › Security › Delete Account**, then **Hold to Delete (2s)**. The key and everything Pombo keeps for the account on this device are deleted. Without the key or a [backup](/use/backup/), the account cannot come back.
